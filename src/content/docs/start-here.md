Slipwatch is a Jira Cloud app for due dates. It records every due-date change in a project: who changed it, when, and by how many days. It can also ask people why they changed a date, or make sure a date changes only with a reason.

## What brings you here?

- **"I want to see who moved our deadlines"**: [Get your first report](/watch/get-your-first-report/)
- **"I want people to say why when they move a date"**: [Turn on Ask](/ask/turn-on-ask/)
- **"No date may change without a reason"**: [Set up Enforce](/enforce/set-up-enforce/)
- **"Slipwatch asked me for a reason"**: [When Slipwatch asks you for a reason](/ask/when-slipwatch-asks-you-for-a-reason/)
- **"I can't change a due date"**: [Change a due date when it's locked](/enforce/change-a-due-date-when-its-locked/)

Have a question? See [Common questions](/help/common-questions/).
